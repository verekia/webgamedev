## 🔥 Headlines

🎾 [**Box3D**](https://box2d.org/posts/2026/06/announcing-box3d/)
([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jr_Fzl2XwKU), [repo](https://github.com/erincatto/box3d),
[docs](https://box2d.org/documentation3d/)) is the new 3D physics engine by [Erin
Catto](https://x.com/erin_catto), the creator of Box2D. It brings the Box2D architecture to three
dimensions with a C API, continuous collision detection, multi-threading, and cross-platform determinism.
[**box3d.js**](https://isaac-mason.github.io/box3d.js/) ([repo](https://github.com/isaac-mason/box3d.js))
by [Isaac Mason](https://x.com/isaac_mason_) already ships WASM bindings with TypeScript types.

🔠 [**Glyph**](https://github.com/pmndrs/glyph)
([announcement](https://x.com/pmndrs/status/2100980802091708818)) is a GPU-first typography engine
for the web by [Justin Walsh](https://x.com/thejustinwalsh) from
[Poimandres](https://github.com/pmndrs). Bitmap, MSDF, and Slug fonts in one engine, with correct
multilingual shaping (bidi, CJK, ligatures), justified paragraphs that wrap around shapes, and a
lot more. It integrates with Three.js, R3F, TresJS, TypeGPU, or your own renderer.

🧮 [**math**](https://github.com/pmndrs/math) ([docs](https://pmndrs.github.io/math/docs/),
[examples](https://pmndrs.github.io/math/examples/),
[announcement](https://x.com/pmndrs/status/2103487633502474300)) is Poimandres' new math engine by [Isaac Mason](https://x.com/isaac_mason_). Allocation-free, monomorphic, tree-shakable, and renderer-agnostic, it covers vectors, quaternions, and matrices, shapes and
spatial queries, noise, easing and springs, IK, colors, and seeded RNGs. And it's simply
`npm i math`!

🎓 [**NoGraphicsAPI**](https://github.com/sebbbi/NoGraphicsAPI)
([announcement](https://x.com/SebAaltonen/status/2096312811743740178)) by [Sebastian
Aaltonen](https://x.com/SebAaltonen) implements his [No Graphics
API](https://www.sebastianaaltonen.com/blog/no-graphics-api) blog post as an MIT-licensed C++20
library on Vulkan 1.4 and Metal 4: buffer objects, descriptor sets, and vertex layouts give way to
GPU pointers and bindless heap indices. Not for the browser, but a clear picture of where GPU APIs
are heading.
