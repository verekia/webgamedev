## 🔥 Headlines

📦 [**Box3D**](https://box2d.org/posts/2026/06/announcing-box3d/) ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jr_Fzl2XwKU), [repo](https://github.com/erincatto/box3d), [docs](https://box2d.org/documentation3d/)) is the new 3D physics engine by [Erin Catto](https://x.com/erin_catto), the creator of Box2D. It brings the Box2D architecture to three dimensions: a plain C API, a sub-stepping soft-constraint solver, continuous collision detection, triangle mesh and height field collision, baked compound shapes, multi-threading, and cross-platform determinism. Erin wrote it while working on [The Legend of California](https://www.kintsugiyama.com/game) at [Kintsugiyama](https://www.kintsugiyama.com/), where the game needs to simulate hundreds of thousands of server-side entities. Being a small C library, it travels to the web well: [**box3d.js**](https://isaac-mason.github.io/box3d.js/) ([repo](https://github.com/isaac-mason/box3d.js)) by [Isaac Mason](https://x.com/isaac_mason_) already ships WASM bindings with TypeScript types and a nice set of examples.

  ([changelog](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/releases), [migration
  guide](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/wiki/Migration-Guide))
- <R3FLogo /> [**R3F v10
  Alpha**](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-fiber/releases/tag/v10.0.0-alpha.4) – The march
  toward first-class WebGPU and TSL support: new TSL hooks, multiple canvases sharing one renderer
  and GPU context, and a standalone [scheduler](https://github.com/pmndrs/scheduler) with named
  phases and FPS limits
- <R3FLogo /> [**Drei v11 Alpha**](https://github.com/pmndrs/drei/releases) – WebGPU groundwork,
  MeshDiscardMaterial, BlurPass converted to TSL, and pixel-perfect Html
- <ThrelteLogo /> [**Threlte Core
  8.6.0**](https://github.com/threlte/threlte/releases/tag/%40threlte%2Fcore%408.6.0) – New
  `@threlte/core/webgpu` export
- <TresLogo /> [**TresJS**](https://tresjs.org/) –
  [@tresjs/rapier](https://github.com/Tresjs/tres/releases/tag/%40tresjs%2Frapier%401.0.0) is out of
  alpha, and [1.1.0](https://github.com/Tresjs/tres/releases/tag/%40tresjs%2Frapier%401.1.0) imports
  glTF physics colliders from node names. [core
  5.9.0](https://github.com/Tresjs/tres/releases/tag/%40tresjs%2Fcore%405.9.0) adds
  MeshPortalMaterial and TresPortal, and [cientos
  5.9.0](https://github.com/Tresjs/tres/releases/tag/%40tresjs%2Fcientos%405.9.0) adds Instances,
  Merged, and Refractor
- <PlayCanvasLogo /> [**PlayCanvas Engine
  v2.21**](https://x.com/playcanvas/status/2079571937303445761) and
  [**v2.22**](https://x.com/playcanvas/status/2095840316389466186) – Gaussian splat refraction and
  depth effects, water rendering, volumetric fog and light shafts, parallax occlusion mapping, and
  vertex animation textures
- <PhaserLogo /> [**Phaser 4.2.1**](https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser/releases/tag/v4.2.1) –
  Stencil, ScaleManager, and ESM build fixes
- <PixiLogo /> [**PixiJS v8.20**](https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs/releases/tag/v8.20.0) and
  [**v8.21**](https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs/releases/tag/v8.21.0) – WGSL shader overrides, WebGPU
  render bundles, depth texture copies, lazy backend imports, and TypeScript 6 and 7 support
- <BabylonLogo /> [**Babylon.js 9.27**](https://github.com/BabylonJS/Babylon.js/releases) – OpenUSD
  WebAssembly scene loader, object ID geometry textures, IBL shadows on ShadowOnlyMaterial, and a
  GUI FlexPanel ([what's new](https://doc.babylonjs.com/whats-new/))
- <NeedleLogo /> [**Needle Engine 6.0
  Alpha**](https://github.com/needle-tools/needle-engine-support/releases) – Experimental
  GaussianSplat component powered by Spark, WebXR anchors enabled by default, and Three.js r185
- 🤺 [**Rapier
  0.35**](https://github.com/dimforge/rapier/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md#v0350-08-august-2026) – SIMD
  always on, sleeping rewritten around persistent islands, and a broad phase that no longer floods
  the narrow phase (up to 90x faster on collider-heavy bodies)
- <ConstructLogo /> [**Construct
  r495**](https://www.construct.net/en/make-games/releases/stable/r495) – 3D rotation for 3D shapes
